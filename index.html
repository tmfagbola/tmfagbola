Who I Am

Bridging Intelligence,
Education & Impact

I am Dr Temitayo Matthew Fagbola, a Teaching Fellow (Assistant Professor) at the Centre of Excellence for Data Science, Artificial Intelligence and Modelling (DAIM), University of Hull, United Kingdom. My work sits at the intersection of applied AI research, pedagogical innovation, and global academic leadership.

My journey began in Nigeria in 2012, where I spent a decade teaching, building, and leading at the Federal University Oye-Ekiti (FUOYE) — pioneering the postgraduate programme in Computer Science, developing computer-based testing systems, and mentoring junior colleagues. A postdoctoral fellowship at Durban University of Technology, South Africa, deepened my global academic perspective and research in inclusive AI pedagogy.

Since joining the University of Hull in October 2022, I have led the Understanding Artificial Intelligence module for cohorts of 500+ students, co-developed the Applied Artificial Intelligence and Big Data modules, secured PhD cluster funding, and continued to produce research on human-centred AI, medical image synthesis, and AI ethics.

I hold a Fellowship of the Higher Education Academy (FHEA) awarded 2024, a Senior Fellowship (SFHEA), a Postgraduate Certificate in Academic Practice (PCAP) from the University of Hull, and a PhD in Computer Science from Ladoke Akintola University of Technology (LAUTECH), Nigeria (2015).

🧠 Teaching Philosophy

"My teaching philosophy is directed towards a learner-centred environment that promotes active learning, inclusivity, and the practical application of knowledge — a 'learning-by-doing-together' approach."

🔄
Active & Team-Based Learning (TBL) Learners work in small groups on large, open-ended, real-world problems — grounded in Team-Based Learning and Kolb's Experiential Learning Theory to foster engagement, critical thinking, and interdisciplinary collaboration.
🎯
Authentic, Industry-Relevant Assessment Summative assessments are portfolio-based (technical report + coded solution), open-ended, industry-sourced, and designed to develop lifelong AI and data science skills rather than test rote recall.
🌍
Inclusive & Culturally Responsive Every learner's identity, background, and experience is acknowledged and integrated into learning activities. Equal opportunity in discussion, identity-inclusive examples, and a dedicated MS Teams community for all students.
⚡
Real-Time Feedback & Student Support Formative assessments (quizzes, group exercises, short assignments) enable real-time progress tracking. Timely interventions for at-risk students — drawing on ML-based early identification models developed since 2017.
💻
Technology-Enhanced Learning Mentimeter, Panopto, Canvas quizzes, YouTube tutorials, and Microsoft Teams are woven into every module to create interactive, accessible, and multi-modal learning experiences.

My research applies AI and machine learning to solve complex, real-world problems — from medical imaging to education, climate, and ethical AI deployment.

Medical AI
Generative AI in Medical Image Synthesis & Cancer Prognosis
Diffusion-based models, Convolutional Autoencoders, and Inception architectures for brain tumour classification, colorectal cancer segmentation, and chronic kidney disease diagnosis from noisy medical imagery.
Human-Centred AI
Ethical, Explainable & Conversational AI Models
Investigating responsible deployment of Generative AI in personalised healthcare; building interpretable LLM outputs via knowledge graphs; advancing fairness, accountability, and transparency (FAT*) in algorithmic systems.
NLP
Social NLP: Sentiment, Misinformation & Social Analysis
Topic modelling, public opinion mining, bot-aware sentiment analysis, and social network analysis applied to climate discourse, elections, and social media platforms (#BBNaija, Nigerian elections, Hong Kong protests).
Computer Vision
Low-Resolution Face Recognition & Semantic Segmentation
Pose and illumination-invariant feature descriptors for surveillance video; semantic image segmentation and classification; resolution reconstruction using GAN architectures and algebraic back-projection techniques.
EdTech AI
AI-Driven Learning Analytics & At-Risk Student Identification
Mobile-interfaced ML models using 81+ variables to predict student performance and enable timely intervention. Extended to Nigerian universities — informing data-driven pedagogy and reducing failure rates in programming courses.
Cloud & Edge AI
Cloud & Edge Intelligence in Higher Education
Frameworks for sustainable cloud computing adoption in resource-constrained HEIs; Edge Intelligence for real-time educational analytics; mobile network quality prediction for improved student digital access at UoH.
Active Research Questions
How can agentic AI tutoring systems autonomously adapt pedagogical strategy, pacing, and content scaffolding in real time — without compromising learner agency or academic integrity?
To what extent do large language model reasoning chains reflect genuine pedagogical understanding, and how can we design reliable evaluation benchmarks for AI in educational contexts?
How can multi-agent AI systems coordinate across clinical imaging, patient history, and genomic data to enable earlier, more equitable cancer diagnosis in low-resource healthcare settings?
What governance frameworks and institutional safeguards are required to ensure responsible deployment of agentic AI systems in UK higher education at scale?
